"# EduBridge" 
EduBridge is an innovative platform designed to facilitate the donation, purchase, and exchange of educational materials, including textbooks, study materials, PDFs, and PowerPoint presentations. The platform leverages OCR (Optical Character Recognition)technology for accurate textbook identification, ensuring that students can easily find the right materials. Additionally, it incorporates a review-based catalog system, allowing users to rate and review resources, helping others make informed decisions. Real-time stock updates ensure that available resources are always up to date, while secure payment integration provides a seamless and trustworthy transaction experience. Beyond resource exchange, EduBridge fosters academic networking through its Alumni Connect and study communities, enabling students to connect with alumni for mentor ship,career guidance, and professional networking opportunities. This feature enhances collaboration and knowledge-sharing, bridging the gap between current students and in dustry professionals.To ensure accessibility and inclusivity, EduBridge integrates assistive technologies suchas text-to-speech (TTS), audio-to-text conversion, and a screen reader for visually impaired users. These features provide a more inclusive learning experience, making educational resources accessible to a diverse range of learners. A key compo nent of the platform is its dynamic chatroom, developed using Django,which facilitates structured communication among students, teachers, and alumni. The chat system supports real-time messaging, multimedia sharing, group creation, and role based moderation, ensuring a secure, organized, and productive discussion envi ronment.To enhance engagement, notifications are integrated using Twilio, keeping users informed about new messages, updates, and donation approvals.
